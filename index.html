About Tristian Justice

Tristian Justice is the founder of Happy Life Financial, a nationwide financial services firm committed to helping individuals and families protect their income, preserve their assets, and build lasting wealth.

Licensed since 2012, Tristian has worked with clients across the country, providing tailored life insurance and retirement strategies designed to meet each client’s unique needs and long-term financial goals.

Through partnerships with top-rated carriers, Happy Life Financial offers a wide range of solutions including income protection, mortgage protection, tax-advantaged growth strategies, and guaranteed retirement income.
